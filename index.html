Why now: the agents are coming.

Personal AI agents — Muse, Instinct, and everything behind them — are about to change how your customers find you. Soon they won't Google "plumber near me" and compare listings. They'll say "find me a good plumber for tomorrow morning" — and the agent will decide.

Agents choose the way the best customers do, only faster and more ruthlessly: most reviews, best responses, fastest answer, easiest booking. The shops that win the agent's pick are the shops already winning the human's pick. Same game, higher stakes.

Straight answers

Before you ask.

What can Buoy actually help with?

Buoy covers ten practical services: After Hours, Lean Stack, Full Picture, AI Crew, Get Found, Regulars, Five Stars, Quote Rescue, Full Book, and Call Intel. We audit the whole operation, then tailor the work to the opportunities that can create the most value for your shop.

Is this replacing my staff?

No. “AI employee” means Buoy takes on repetitive, trackable work across the operation. Your people keep the judgment, relationships, and customer moments that need a human.

Does AI talk directly to customers?

Only where you approve it. We start with narrow instructions, clear escalation rules, and human review for anything sensitive or unusual.

Do I need to replace my current software?

Maybe less of it. The audit starts with what you already use, then separates what should be kept, connected, rebuilt simply, or removed. A new subscription has to earn its place.

Why only 10 founding businesses?

The first group gets unusually close support while the service is refined around real local operations. Keeping it small protects the quality of the work.

What happens after the three-month pilot?

You can continue month-to-month at the locked founding rate. If it isn’t useful, we stop — no long agency contract.
