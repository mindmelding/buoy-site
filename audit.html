A 15-minute intro call, then access to look at the things customers touch: your phone system, Google reviews, website, and whatever you use for quotes and scheduling. Read-only wherever possible.

Not when you book. The intro call is free. If we agree you're a fit, we send a $100 invoice and the audit starts the same week. If you're not a fit, there's nothing to pay.

What if I don't continue after the audit?

The report is yours to keep, no strings attached. The $100 is only credited if you move forward with Buoy. Either way, you'll know exactly where the money is leaking.

Is my business a fit?

The audit was built for local service businesses: auto, home services, dental, and similar shops. We'll tell you on the intro call if you're a fit. If you're not, we'll tell you that too.

Will this disrupt my team?

No. The audit is observation only. We don't change a single thing until you've seen the report and approved a plan.
